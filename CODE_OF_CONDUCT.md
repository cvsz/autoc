# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and
professional. Welcome different experience levels, focus criticism on the
work, and assume good intent while still addressing harmful behavior clearly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct without consent, and
publishing another person's private information or credentials.

## Enforcement

Report abusive behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Give
the location, context, and any relevant links; do not include secrets. The
maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, limit participation, or
block accounts when necessary to protect the community.
